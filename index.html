Abstract

An OWL 2 ontology for describing municipal IT incidents, employees, departments, devices and technician assignments.

Introduction back to ToC

This vocabulary was developed by Omar García Carballo as a practical ontology engineering course project. Intended users are municipal helpdesk staff and developers of interoperable incident management systems.

Municipal IT Incident Ontology: Description back to ToC

The ontology supports a small municipal helpdesk. It models incident reporting, affected devices, current status and priority, assignment to technicians and employee department membership. Software applications, network infrastructure, service-level agreements and change management are outside its scope.
